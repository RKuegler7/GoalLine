# Community guidelines

GoalLine is for people helping each other finish what matters. These guidelines apply everywhere in the app: GoalLeagues, Challenges, Highlights posts and comments, Explore, profiles and messages. They sit alongside the [Terms of use](https://ddhxw7holo4vs.cloudfront.net/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](https://ddhxw7holo4vs.cloudfront.net/privacy).

## Be encouraging

- Cheer progress, not just wins. Finishing last in a league while hitting your goal is a win.
- Keep competition friendly. Trash talk that's playful between friends is fine; insults, harassment and piling on are not.
- A slip is one day, not a verdict. Be kind about other people's misses, and your own.

## Be honest

- Check in only what you actually did. GoalLine is scored by code, and it only means something if the check-ins are real.
- Proof photos and details should be your own and show the work they claim.
- Disputes are for real questions, asked politely ("Can you show the route?"), not for gaining an edge. League and Challenge owners settle them fairly.

## Keep it safe

- Don't share anyone's private information: addresses, phone numbers, emails, or photos of other people without their OK.
- Crop maps, house numbers and anything that reveals where you live out of photos. GoalLine removes location data from photos, but not what's visible in them.
- No hate, threats, sexual content, or content that encourages self-harm, disordered eating or unsafe training. Health goals should be pursued safely; GoalLine is not medical advice.
- No spam, ads or impersonation.

## Public spaces

- GoalLeagues, Challenges and posts are private unless their owner makes them public. Public ones can be seen and joined by anyone signed in to GoalLine, so keep public names, descriptions and posts suitable for everyone.
- Owners of public GoalLeagues and Challenges are responsible for keeping them welcoming.

## When something's wrong

- **Report** a post from its menu. It's hidden for you right away and reviewed.
- **Mute** someone so their posts never show for you; unmute from their profile.
- League and Challenge owners can remove members.
- For anything serious, use the feedback form on the About page.

Breaking these guidelines can mean removed posts, removal from a GoalLeague or Challenge, or a closed account.
